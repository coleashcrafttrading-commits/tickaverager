# Options: the rules the machine must obey

193 machine-enforceable rules from deep research, plus what was measured
directly against this Alpaca account. Where the two disagree, the measurement
wins and the difference is noted.

The canonical machine-readable copy is `options/mechanics.json`; this file is
for reading. `optengine.py` is where these become code, and every one of them
that could put the account into a position it cannot exit is a refusal there,
not a comment.

## Measured here, not researched

* **The multi-leg limit price sign is the most dangerous thing in the API.**
  The same put credit spread, sell 600 / buy 595:
  `limit_price "4.90"` was ACCEPTED and held $990 of buying power;
  `limit_price "-4.90"` was ACCEPTED and held $500. Negative is a credit,
  positive is a debit, and $500 is the real max loss on a 5-wide spread --
  the $990 case is Alpaca correctly reserving width plus a $490 debit, because
  a positive price on a credit structure IS an instruction to pay. **Alpaca
  does not reject the wrong sign. It fills it.**
* Level 3 rejects uncovered shorts with HTTP 403 "account not eligible to
  trade uncovered option contracts". 57 of the 231 banked strategies need
  level 4.
* Multi-leg is 2 to 4 legs; outside that is a 422.
* Option market orders are rejected outside 09:30-16:00 ET. Limit orders,
  day or GTC, are accepted while the market is closed and rest until the open.
* Alpaca returns no greeks and no implied volatility, ever, at any feed.
* `/v2/options/contracts` silently returns only the nearest expiry unless
  `expiration_date_gte` is passed, and never returns an expired contract.
* Market data (10,000/min) is a separate rate-limit budget from trading
  (200/min, shared with the share fleet).

## US equity/ETF option assignment, exercise and settlement mechanics for a never-assigned automated system (Alpaca, options level 3, paper, American-style share-settled)

1. FLATTEN DEADLINE (hard): no short leg on any American-style, share-settled option may be open after 15:00:00 ET on its expiration date. At 14:45 ET submit the closing order; at 14:55 ET if unfilled, reprice to marketable; at 15:05 ET if still unfilled, send a market order; at 15:12 ET if still open, raise a critical page. Rationale: Alpaca rejects 0DTE orders after 15:15 ET for single-name options and 15:30 ET for broad-based ETFs, and stops accepting opening/extending orders at 15:30 ET.
2. ALL DEADLINES FROM THE EXCHANGE CALENDAR: compute every expiration-day deadline as (session_close - 60 min), never as a hard-coded wall-clock time. On a 13:00 ET half-day close the flatten deadline is 12:00 ET. Reject the trading day entirely if the calendar lookup fails.
3. NEVER CALL THE EXERCISE ENDPOINT. POST /v2/positions/{id}/exercise has no quantity parameter and exercises the whole position. Remediate any assigned share position with ordinary equity orders instead. Assert this at the HTTP-client layer: block the URL pattern.
4. NEVER DEPEND ON DO-NOT-EXERCISE. Alpaca DNE is submitted by email and processed manually, best-efforts after 16:00 ET. No code path may branch on 'submit DNE'. It is a human escape hatch only.
5. CLOSE SPREADS ATOMICALLY: exit a multi-leg position with a single mleg order. If the broker rejects the mleg close and leg-by-leg is required, close the SHORT leg first and only then the long leg. Never allow a state where the long leg is closed and the short leg is open -- assert this invariant after every fill and halt the strategy if it is violated.
6. NO 0DTE SHORT LEGS unless explicitly enabled per-underlying. If enabled, no new short leg may be opened on an expiring contract after 11:00 ET, and the 15:00 ET flatten rule still applies with no exception for profitability.
7. PIN-RISK BAND: at 14:30 ET on expiration day, for every short strike K with underlying price S, if |S - K| <= max(0.005 * S, $0.50), flatten the entire spread immediately at market regardless of P&L. Re-evaluate every 60 seconds until 15:00 ET; the band widens rather than narrows as the clock runs.
8. ASSIGNMENT-IS-$0.01: everywhere the code asks 'is this leg at risk', the ITM test is (S - K) >= 0.01 for calls and (K - S) >= 0.01 for puts. Do not use a wider tolerance to mean 'safe'.
9. OTM IS NOT SAFE: never mark an expiring short leg as settled/worthless based on the 16:00 ET price. Hold expiring short positions in state PENDING_EXPIRY_CONFIRMATION until the next-session activity poll confirms. No capital freed by a pending expiry may be reused for new positions.
10. EXTRINSIC MONITOR (every cycle, all short legs): extrinsic = option_mid - max(0, S-K) for calls, option_mid - max(0, K-S) for puts. If extrinsic <= $0.05, mark EARLY_ASSIGNMENT_RISK=HIGH and close the spread the same cycle. If the option bid/ask spread exceeds the computed extrinsic, treat the reading as unreliable, mark it STALE, and close on the conservative assumption rather than trusting the number.
11. DIVIDEND GUARD (short calls): run a pre-open job that queries the Alpaca corporate actions announcements API for ca_type=Dividend on every underlying with an open or candidate short call. If ex_date is within the next 2 trading sessions AND the short call is ITM, flatten by 15:00 ET on the session BEFORE ex_date. Additionally flatten if extrinsic < dividend_per_share regardless of moneyness.
12. DIVIDEND GUARD FAILS CLOSED: if the corporate-actions lookup errors, returns empty for a symbol not on a verified no-dividend whitelist, or returns data older than 2 days, treat the symbol as having an unknown ex-date and block any new short call on it. Alpaca's announcement feed has documented ingestion lag and gaps.
13. NO SHORT CALLS INTO EX-DATE: hard block on opening any short call leg on a dividend-paying underlying within 5 sessions of a known ex_date unless extrinsic value at entry exceeds 3x the expected dividend per share.
14. DEEP-ITM PUT FLOOR: close any short put whose extrinsic <= $0.05, at any DTE. Do not attempt to model the interest-rate-driven early-exercise boundary -- the system has no risk-free-rate input and the evidence for a numeric trigger is weak.
15. SIZE FOR ASSIGNMENT, NOT FOR SPREAD WIDTH: before opening any spread, verify the account can absorb full assignment of the short leg as a stock position -- strike * 100 * qty of buying-power impact for short puts, and the equivalent short-stock requirement for short calls -- without triggering a margin call. If not, reduce quantity. Never size a position on the assumption that max loss equals spread width.
16. ASSIGNMENT RECONCILIATION JOB: poll GET /v2/account/activities for activity types OPASN, OPEXC, OPEXP and OPTRD at 08:00 ET, 09:00 ET, and every 15 minutes during the session. Do not rely on the trade-updates websocket -- Alpaca does not push assignments over it.
17. ON OPASN DETECTED: immediately (a) halt new order generation for that underlying, (b) flatten the resulting share position at the next available liquid moment, (c) reconcile the ledger against GET /v2/positions as the source of truth rather than internal state, (d) page a human. Handle PARTIAL assignment: the assigned quantity may be less than the position size.
18. BROKER STATE IS TRUTH: before every order-generation cycle, diff internal position state against GET /v2/positions. Alpaca can unilaterally liquidate ITM longs near expiry and can close positions with large dividend-risk exposure the day before ex-date. Any unexplained diff halts trading and pages.
19. ASSIGNMENT LOCK IS NOT RECOVERABLE IN CODE: if orders on an underlying start rejecting after an assignment event, stop retrying after 2 attempts, mark the underlying DISABLED, and page -- the lock is only lifted after a human contacts Alpaca support.
20. PAPER DOES NOT VALIDATE THIS: on paper accounts Alpaca syncs option exercise/assignment/expiry activities only at the start of the following day, and early-assignment simulation is undocumented. Test the OPASN handling path with injected synthetic activity records in an integration test; never treat a clean paper run as evidence the assignment path works.
21. IF 'NEVER ASSIGNED' IS ABSOLUTE, PREFER CASH-SETTLED EUROPEAN INDEX OPTIONS (SPX/SPXW/XSP/VIX on Alpaca): no early exercise, no share delivery. Note these are paper-only on Alpaca today, and that AM-settled monthlies settle off opening prints, so an expiration-morning exit is not possible for them -- flatten the session before.
22. DAILY INVARIANT ASSERTION at 15:20 ET: count of open short legs on share-settled options expiring today must equal zero. If not zero, the system is in a failed state: page immediately and do not start the next session's strategy logic until a human clears it.

## Risk management and position sizing for a fully automated multi-strategy options book (Alpaca, options level 3, ~$50k paper account, US equities/ETFs, American-style share-settled)

1. SIZING -- single risk number: For every candidate position compute defined_max_loss locally before submission. For a vertical: (width_in_points * 100 * contracts) - (net_credit * 100 * contracts). For any multi-leg, evaluate the piecewise payoff at every strike and at 0 and 2x spot, and take |worst net payoff|. Reject any position for which this number cannot be computed in closed form. Never size from premium collected.
2. SIZING -- per-position cap: defined_max_loss <= 1.0% of start-of-day account equity. At $50k that is $500 per position. Hard reject above; no override flag in code. Rationale: this is a judgement call, not a derived optimum -- it is chosen so that ~30 consecutive maximum losses (the empirical PUT-index drawdown scale) does not end the account.
3. SIZING -- per-underlying cap: sum of defined_max_loss across all open positions on one underlying <= 2.0% of equity ($1,000 at $50k). Aggregate by underlying symbol, not by contract.
4. SIZING -- total deployed cap: sum of defined_max_loss across the entire book <= 10% of equity ($5,000 at $50k). Reject any new order that would breach it. This is the binding constraint most of the time and should be the first check.
5. SIZING -- never Kelly as the sizer: Do not compute position size from the Kelly criterion. If a Kelly estimate is computed at all, it may only be used as a multiplier in [0, 0.25] applied to the cap above, may never increase size above the cap, and must be disabled until at least 300 closed trades exist on the same strategy and underlying. Kelly under normal assumptions oversizes negatively-skewed payoffs.
6. SIZING -- no martingale: Position size must be a pure function of current equity and the caps above. It must never reference recent P&L, loss streaks, or 'recovery' targets. Assert in code that the sizing function does not read the trade history.
7. SIZING -- minimum viable size: If the computed contract count rounds to 0, do not open the position. Never round up to 1.
8. GREEKS -- compute locally: Alpaca returns no greeks or IV on 0DTE at any tier. Compute all greeks locally using intraday time-to-expiry in years (not whole days). If a greek cannot be computed for any open or candidate leg, treat the portfolio greek check as FAILED, not as zero.
9. GREEKS -- beta-weighted net delta: Beta-weight every position's delta to SPY and cap |net portfolio beta-weighted delta| at 0.30 * (equity / SPY_price) shares-equivalent -- at $50k with SPY near $600 that is about +/-25 SPY-equivalent deltas, i.e. a 1% SPY move moves the book about $150. Reject any order that would breach it. This cap is a convention with no authoritative source; log it as a tunable.
10. GREEKS -- net vega: Cap net portfolio vega at -0.10% of equity per vol point (about -$50 per 1 vol point at $50k) and at +0.05% on the long side. A short-premium book will run short vega; the cap exists so a VIX move of 20 points (observed twice in the last decade: +20 pts on 2018-02-05, +40 pts on 2024-08-05) costs at most ~$1,000 in vega terms.
11. GREEKS -- gamma: Cap |net portfolio gamma| such that the change in net beta-weighted delta from a 2% adverse SPY move stays inside the delta cap above. Implement as: simulate spot -2% and +2%, recompute net delta, reject the order if either simulated net delta breaches the delta cap.
12. GREEKS -- theta is not a limit: Do not set a minimum theta target. A theta floor is an incentive to sell more premium and inverts the purpose of the risk system. Report theta; never gate on it.
13. CONCURRENCY -- max concurrent positions: <= 8 open multi-leg positions total, and <= 3 on any single underlying. Count a multi-leg position as one position. These are operational limits (what the machine can monitor and exit within one polling cycle), not statistical ones.
14. CONCURRENCY -- correlation is not diversification: Positions on different underlyings do not relax the total-deployed cap. Do not implement any rule that increases allowed size because positions are on different symbols.
15. CONCURRENCY -- allowlist only: Trade only symbols on an explicit, human-maintained allowlist stored in config. Reject every symbol not on it, including symbols returned by a scanner. No dynamic universe expansion at runtime.
16. DAILY LOSS -- key to broker equity: At the first successful poll of each session, snapshot account equity from Alpaca's trading account endpoint to durable storage with the date. All intraday loss checks compare current broker-reported equity to that snapshot, never to internally computed P&L.
17. DAILY LOSS -- soft limit: If (snapshot_equity - current_equity) >= 2% of snapshot_equity, stop opening new positions for the rest of the session. Managing and closing existing positions continues.
18. DAILY LOSS -- hard kill switch: If (snapshot_equity - current_equity) >= 4% of snapshot_equity, cancel all working orders, stop opening anything, write a latched HALTED state to disk with timestamp and reason, and alert a human. Do not auto-liquidate -- liquidating into a stressed tape is itself a loss event.
19. KILL SWITCH -- durability: The halted state must be written to a file or database, not held in memory. On every startup, read it BEFORE connecting to the broker or evaluating any strategy. A halt cleared by a restart is not a halt.
20. KILL SWITCH -- human clearance: The hard halt may only be cleared by a human action (a file edit, a CLI command with an explicit confirmation, or a dashboard button). No time-based auto-reset, no auto-clear at midnight.
21. KILL SWITCH -- additional trip conditions, each latching the halt: (a) more than 3 order rejections from Alpaca in 60 seconds; (b) any unexpected share position appearing in the account (assignment); (c) market data staleness > 60 seconds during RTH; (d) any account restriction flag from Alpaca (account_blocked, trading_blocked, transfers_blocked, or a liquidation-only restriction); (e) an unhandled exception in the main loop.
22. WATCHDOG -- process liveness is a risk control: Alpaca supports no bracket or OTO orders for options and no GTC -- every exit is managed by this process. Run a watchdog that alerts a human if the main loop has not completed a cycle in 120 seconds during RTH. Document a manual flatten procedure that a human can run without the bot.
23. PROFIT TAKING -- 50% target, net of the real exit cost: Close a credit spread when it can be closed at or below 50% of the credit received, evaluated against the NATURAL price (what it would actually cost to close), not the mid. Implement as a limit order at 50% of credit, repriced toward the natural on a schedule, with a maximum giveup. Treat 50% as a config parameter with a logged default, not a constant.
24. PROFIT TAKING -- evidence caveat in code: Annotate the 50% and 21-DTE parameters in config with a comment stating that their published support is a single vendor's proprietary backtests without independent replication, and that the machine logs its own realized outcomes so the parameters can be re-fit on this account's data.
25. TIME STOP -- 21 DTE: Close any short-premium position at 21 calendar days to expiration regardless of P&L, using a limit order with a defined giveup. The mechanism (gamma rises, theta compensation falls in the final weeks) is sound options math; the specific number 21 is a convention. If a 21-DTE close cannot be filled inside the giveup, escalate to alert rather than crossing the spread.
26. STOP LOSS -- express as credit multiple, but as a limit and with a liquidity guard: If a stop is enabled, trigger when the spread's mark reaches 2.0x the credit received (i.e. an unrealized loss of 1.0x credit). Submit a limit order, never a market order. SKIP the stop entirely and hold to the defined max loss if the spread's quoted bid/ask is wider than 20% of the spread's mark, or if either leg has no bid. A stop that pays an unbounded spread is worse than the bounded loss it was meant to avoid.
27. STOP LOSS -- never on a single leg: Evaluate and exit the position as a whole via an MLeg order. Never close one leg of a vertical, which converts a defined-risk position into an undefined-risk one.
28. GAP HANDLING -- separate code path: When the underlying has gapped through a short strike (spread mark >= 80% of max loss at the first poll of the session), do NOT run the normal stop. Instead: (1) immediately set the no-new-positions flag for the session; (2) compute the position's theoretical value from local greeks; (3) attempt a limit close with a maximum giveup of 5% of the spread width, repricing at most 3 times over 10 minutes; (4) if unfilled, hold, alert a human, and re-evaluate next cycle. Log every attempt. It cannot be determined from available data whether holding or exiting is better after a gap; the rule optimizes for not paying the widest spread of the day and for putting a human in the loop.
29. EXPIRY -- close before the broker's cutoff, with margin: Close or roll every expiring position no later than 3:00 pm ET on expiration day for single equities and 3:15 pm ET for SPY/QQQ. Alpaca rejects orders after 3:15 pm ET (equities) and 3:30 pm ET (broad-based ETFs) and takes control of expiring positions from 3:30 pm ET. Never schedule an action inside the broker's window.
30. EXPIRY -- pin-risk rule: Never carry a short leg into expiration when |spot - short_strike| < 1.0% of spot. OCC exercise-by-exception is $0.01 ITM, the exercise-notice deadline is 4:30 pm CT (after the underlying stops trading), brokers may use different thresholds, and holders may choose either way. Close the position instead of predicting assignment.
31. ASSIGNMENT -- detect every cycle: Poll the account activities endpoint for OPASN, OPEXC and OPEXP events every cycle and reconcile positions against the broker's reported positions, not against internal state. Any share position the machine did not intend is an assignment.
32. ASSIGNMENT -- flatten delivered shares same session: On detecting assignment, immediately flatten the resulting share position during regular hours (there are no extended hours for options at Alpaca, and share risk overnight is unbounded). Then close or re-evaluate the orphaned long leg. Never hold assigned shares overnight to 'let it come back'.
33. ASSIGNMENT -- dividend blackout: Do not open, and close if already open, any position with a short call that is ITM or within 2% of the money when the underlying's ex-dividend date is within 2 trading days. Assignment before ex-div obligates paying 100 x dividend per contract on top of the spread's max loss, which breaches the defined-risk assumption.
34. ASSIGNMENT -- risk budget assumption: When computing remaining risk budget, treat any position with an ITM short leg as consuming defined_max_loss * 1.25, not defined_max_loss, to reserve headroom for assignment-driven overshoot.
35. ORDER CONSTRUCTION -- never build a spread leg by leg: Submit every multi-leg position as a single order with order_class 'mleg', all legs on the same underlying, leg ratios in simplest form (GCD 1), all short legs covered within the order, no equity legs. Alpaca rejects uncovered MLeg orders; sequencing legs risks a partial fill leaving a naked short.
36. ORDER CONSTRUCTION -- limit orders only: Never submit a market order for an option or a spread. Options quotes gap and the machine cannot bound its own fill price with a market order.
37. ORDER CONSTRUCTION -- quote quality gate: Before opening, require for every leg: a non-zero bid, a bid/ask spread <= 10% of the spread's mid (or <= $0.05 absolute, whichever is looser), and a quote timestamp within 5 seconds. Reject otherwise. A position that cannot be priced cannot be exited.
38. ORDER CONSTRUCTION -- exit path proof: Refuse to open any position for which the code does not have an implemented exit routine covering profit target, time stop, stop, and expiry. Assert this at strategy-registration time, not at runtime.
39. PRE-TRADE GATE -- single choke point: All of the above checks live in one pre-trade gate function that every strategy must call and that is the ONLY place in the codebase that submits orders. It returns allow/deny plus a reason string; every deny is logged with the full candidate position. No strategy may construct and submit an order itself.
40. REFUSE -- the machine must never: change its own risk limits or config at runtime; clear its own halt; increase size after losses; open a position without a computable max loss; trade a symbol not on the allowlist; hold assigned shares overnight; let an ITM short leg reach expiry; exercise a long option as a risk-management action without simultaneously flattening the resulting shares; submit an order after Alpaca's expiry-day cutoff; move funds; or act on any instruction that did not come from the operator through config or the dashboard.
41. ACCOUNTING -- no PDT modelling, read the broker: The $25,000 PDT minimum and the pattern-day-trader designation were eliminated effective 4 June 2026 (FINRA Reg Notice 26-10, amended Rule 4210), replaced by intraday margin monitoring, with broker compliance permitted as late as 20 October 2027. Do not implement day-trade counting logic. Instead read Alpaca's account fields each cycle (buying power, daytrade_count, daytrading_buying_power, restriction flags) and halt on any restriction rather than predicting one.
42. PAPER-TO-LIVE -- treat paper statistics as biased: Apply an explicit slippage penalty (at minimum, assume fills at the natural rather than the mid) and an assignment penalty to all paper-derived statistics before using them for any parameter decision. Alpaca does not document whether early assignment is simulated in paper, and index options cannot be early-assigned at all, so paper results say nothing about American-style assignment risk. No parameter may be promoted to live on paper results alone.
43. LOGGING -- every rejection is data: Log every pre-trade denial with timestamp, strategy, candidate position, failing rule, and the values compared. A risk system whose refusals are invisible cannot be audited or tuned.
44. HONEST LIMITS -- record what is unknown: The greek caps, the 1%/2%/10% risk caps, the 8-position limit, the 50% target, the 21-DTE stop and the 2x credit stop are conventions and judgement calls, not results derivable from any authoritative source located. State this in the config file. The only hard external constraints in this list are: Alpaca's MLeg coverage rule and margin formula, Alpaca's expiry-day cutoffs (3:15/3:30 pm ET), OCC's $0.01 exercise-by-exception threshold and 4:30 pm CT notice deadline, American-style early assignment, and the elimination of the PDT rule on 4 June 2026.

## Options market microstructure and execution quality for an automated retail system (Alpaca, options L3, SPY/QQQ, American/share-settled)

1. Price every order from the NBBO only. Compute mid = (bid + ask) / 2 from the current quote. Never read, store or branch on last trade price, close price, or mark for any pricing, stop, or fill decision. If a code path needs a 'price' and no two-sided quote exists, it must raise, not fall back to last.
2. Reject the quote and do not trade if ANY of: bid <= 0; ask <= 0; bid >= ask (crossed or locked); quote timestamp older than 2 seconds during RTH (5 seconds if using the indicative feed); the quote arrived outside 09:30:00-16:00:00 ET. Log the reason code.
3. Snap every limit price to the contract's own tick. Read the tick from instrument metadata at order time; do not assume $0.01. Rounding direction must always be against yourself (buy rounds down to the tick unless that makes the order non-marketable by design; sell rounds up). Assert (price * 100) % (tick * 100) == 0 before submit or refuse to send.
4. Never submit a market order for an option, single-leg or multi-leg, under any condition including forced exits. Forced exits use an aggressive limit at or through the opposite side with an explicit cap, never type='market'.
5. Gate entry on spread as a percent of mid, not dollars. Hard caps: reject if (ask - bid) / mid > 0.10 for DTE >= 7, > 0.06 for DTE 1-6, > 0.15 for 0DTE. Additionally reject any contract whose mid < $0.10 for opening trades -- at that price one tick is >=10% and the spread dominates any edge.
6. Gate entry on displayed size, separately from volume and OI: require bid_size >= 10 and ask_size >= 10, and require the side you must eventually trade out of to show at least 3x your intended contract count. A high-volume, high-OI contract with 2 contracts on the offer fails this gate.
7. Gate entry on open interest >= 500 and same-day volume >= 500 on every leg. Treat these as necessary but not sufficient: they never override the live spread and size gates, which are computed from the current quote.
8. Use order_class='mleg' for every multi-leg structure. Legging in (submitting leg orders separately) is forbidden in code -- there must be no code path that opens a defined-risk spread as two independent orders. If a combo will not fill, walk the net price or abandon the trade.
9. For mleg limit_price, positive = net debit, negative = net credit. Route all signing through one helper function; before submit, assert that sign(limit_price) equals the sign the strategy declared for that structure, and abort on mismatch. Unit-test both directions.
10. Respect Alpaca's mleg constraints as pre-submit validations: no equity legs; every short leg must be covered inside the same order; reduce all leg ratio_qty by their GCD so the GCD across legs is 1. Validate locally and fail fast rather than relying on the broker rejection.
11. Execution ladder for entries: start at the mid rounded to the tick in your favor; if unfilled after T seconds, reprice one tick toward the opposite side; repeat up to N steps; cap the total concession at a configured fraction of the spread (default: half the spread for DTE>=1, full spread for 0DTE exits only). Defaults: T = 3 seconds, N = 4. Abandon the trade if the cap is reached without a fill; never chase past the cap.
12. Implement the ladder as cancel/replace of the whole order, because Alpaca options support only DAY and GTC (no IOC, no FOK) and mleg child legs cannot be cancelled or replaced individually. Every cancel must handle the 'already filled' race: re-query order state after cancel and reconcile positions before submitting the replacement. Never submit a replacement before confirming the prior order is terminal.
13. Use time_in_force='day' for all options orders. Never use GTC on an options order -- a resting GTC limit can fill days later on a quote the strategy never evaluated.
14. Treat bid == 0 as an explicit state named NO_BID, not as price zero. A long position in a NO_BID contract cannot be exited: do not compute a mid, do not attempt a sell, log it and route to the expiry handler. Never mark a position at mid when bid == 0.
15. Blackout windows: no new entries in the first 5 minutes after 09:30 ET (series open on the first underlying print after 9:30, off a queuing book) and no new entries after 15:45 ET. Exits are permitted in both windows.
16. Close or roll every position expiring today no later than 15:00 ET. Never hold an ITM long option into the last hour: Alpaca auto-exercises anything ITM by $0.01 and force-sells positions it cannot fund within 1 hour of expiry, at prices you do not control. Before 15:00, check exercise cost = strike * 100 * contracts against buying power and force the exit if it exceeds it.
17. Backtests and the paper-to-live promotion gate must debit modeled slippage; broker-reported paper P&L is not admissible. Alpaca paper fills at the NBBO with no size check, no market impact and no latency slippage. Per leg per fill, charge: base case = max(half_spread rounded up to one tick, 1 tick); pessimistic case = full spread for 0DTE and for any fill after 15:30 ET. Report P&L under base and pessimistic; require the strategy to be profitable under pessimistic before any live capital.
18. For multi-leg structures, model entry cost as the sum of per-leg half-spreads, and run a sensitivity at the sum of per-leg FULL spreads. Do not assume the combo fills at the net mid.
19. Record, for every order, the full quote snapshot that justified it (bid, ask, sizes, timestamp, feed name) and the actual fill price. Compute realized slippage = |fill - mid_at_decision| per leg and store it. After 200 fills, recalibrate the backtest slippage constants from this table; until then the constants are assumptions, not measurements.
20. Log which data feed produced each quote. If the account is on the Basic/indicative feed, multiply every spread threshold tolerance by 0.5 (be stricter) and set the staleness limit to 5 seconds -- Alpaca describes indicative quotes as 'adjusted', and the derivation is undocumented, so they are not the NBBO.
21. Do not increase position size, unlock additional risk, or treat a strategy as validated on the basis of a single anomalously good fill. A fill priced far from the pre-trade NBBO may be nullified or repriced under exchange obvious/catastrophic error rules; quarantine such fills from sizing logic until the next session confirms them.
22. Kill switch: if the fraction of orders that reach the ladder cap without filling exceeds 50% over the last 20 attempts, or if realized slippage over the last 20 fills exceeds 2x the modeled base case, halt new entries and require human review. Persistent non-fill or excess slippage means the liquidity assumptions are wrong, not that the bot should try harder.

## Honest backtesting of options strategies from 1-minute OHLCV bars and trade prints with no historical bid/ask quotes (SPY/QQQ, Alpaca, options level 3)

1. DATA PROVENANCE: every stored bar/trade row must carry feed ('opra'|'indicative') and fetch timestamp. Any backtest that touches a row with feed != 'opra' aborts with an error naming the rows. Indicative data is never an input to a number that appears in a report.
2. NO CLOSE FILLS: the fill engine must have no code path that assigns bar.close (or vwap, open, high, low) as a fill price. Enforce with a test that mutates all close values by +50% and asserts every simulated fill price changes only through the modelled-quote path.
3. QUOTE MODEL REQUIRED: every fill price = round_to_tick(mid_hat +/- half_spread), buys at the ask side, sells at the bid side, never the mid. half_spread comes from the configured spread model; mid_hat comes from the repricing model, not from the last print of the decision bar.
4. TICK GRID: for SPY/QQQ/IWM round all modelled bids/asks to $0.01 at every premium and enforce quoted_spread >= $0.01 (half_spread >= $0.005). For any other underlying use $0.05 below $3.00 / $0.10 above unless that symbol's penny-program membership on that date is recorded in a data file with a source URL.
5. SPREAD FLOOR IN DOLLARS: assert modelled round-trip spread cost per contract per leg >= $1.00 for SPY/QQQ. Any configuration producing less fails validation.
6. CALIBRATION FLAG: the spread model config must have fields {calibrated_from: dataset id, date_range, n_observations} or calibrated_from='ASSUMPTION'. When it is 'ASSUMPTION', the run sets report.assumed_cost=true and every output file's header says ASSUMED-COST, NOT CALIBRATED.
7. COST SENSITIVITY IS MANDATORY: every run computes results at 1x, 2x and 4x half_spread. If the sign of net P&L flips by 4x, mark result 'not robust'. If it flips by 2x, mark 'not established' and block any promotion to a live or paper deployment path.
8. ESTIMATOR GATING - ROLL: compute only where the first-order autocovariance is negative; report the undefined fraction; if undefined_fraction > 0.20 the estimator output is discarded (not zero-filled, not averaged). Negative estimates are never truncated to zero and then averaged.
9. ESTIMATOR GATING - CORWIN-SCHULTZ: require the bar/window to have >= 20 prints and high > low; report the fraction of windows failing; if > 0.30 fail, disable the estimator for that contract-day. Prefer EDGE (Ardia/Guidotti/Kroencke) over Roll and CS where an OHLC estimator is used at all.
10. ESTIMATORS ARE LOWER BOUNDS ONLY: half_spread_used = max(estimator_output, tick_floor, parametric_model_output). No code path may use an estimator output directly as the fill spread.
11. NO ESTIMATION ON CONTAMINATED PRINTS: if the data source does not expose OPRA trade condition codes (Alpaca aggregated bars do not), complex/multi-leg legs cannot be excluded -- so Roll/CS/EDGE must not be run on that source at all; the spread is recorded as 'unknown' and the parametric floor model is used.
12. ZERO-PRINT BARS: when a bar has trade_count == 0 or the last print is > 60s stale, mid_hat must be produced by theoretical repricing from the underlying's 1-minute bar plus an IV input, never by last-observation-carried-forward. Tag every such fill synthetic_mid=true.
13. SYNTHETIC-FILL DISCLOSURE: the run summary must report the share of simulated fills in bars with 0 prints and with < 5 prints. If > 10% of fills are in zero-print bars, the result is labelled 'unverifiable fills' and cannot be cited as evidence for or against the strategy.
14. UNIVERSE FROM THE BROKER, NOT FROM A FORMULA: only contract symbols returned by the contracts endpoint (queried with expiration_date_gte/lte covering the backtest window, status including inactive) may enter the tradable universe. A synthesized OSI string may be used to QUERY but never to TRADE. Log count of synthesized symbols rejected for lack of listing evidence.
15. LISTING EVIDENCE != PRINT EVIDENCE: maintain two separate exclusion counters, no_listing_evidence and zero_prints. A contract may be excluded for the first reason; it must NOT be excluded for the second (absence of prints is not absence of a market). Any code that filters the universe on volume/print-count fails review.
16. SYMBOL VALIDATION: reject any option symbol whose root is not exactly 'SPY' or 'QQQ' (blocks adjusted roots SPY1/QQQ1 with non-standard deliverables), and validate OSI format: 6-char space-padded root, YYMMDD, C or P, strike x 1000 zero-padded to 8 digits. Reject on any mismatch.
17. NO INTRABAR FILLS: stops, take-profits and triggers are evaluated only at bar boundaries against modelled bid/ask. A lint must fail the build if bar.high or bar.low is referenced anywhere inside the fill/exit engine.
18. NO LOOKAHEAD: decisions at bar t may read only data with timestamp <= t_start; the earliest permitted fill is bar t+1. Ship a one-time assertion test that pins the API's bar timestamp convention (start vs end) against a known market event and stores the result in the repo.
19. FEES ON EVERY LEG, BOTH SIDES: charge OCC $0.025 (buy+sell), ORF $0.015 (buy+sell), FINRA TAF $0.00329 (sell), CAT $0.0003 (buy+sell), SEC $0.0000206 x notional (sell). Index options additionally $0.50/contract plus the exchange table. Fee constants live in one file with effective_date and source_url; the run warns if effective_date predates the backtest end by > 12 months. Gross-of-fee equity curves may not be written to any report.
20. EXPIRY IS A REAL EVENT: any option >= $0.01 ITM at settlement that was not explicitly closed becomes an exercise/assignment of 100 shares per contract; model the resulting share position, its margin, and the overnight gap. Never mark an expiring position to a modelled mid and delete it. Count and report 'pin exposures' (|spot - strike| <= $0.05 at 15:59) as an unquantified risk, not a priced one.
21. EARLY ASSIGNMENT: for short ITM calls, flag every position held through an underlying ex-dividend date where remaining extrinsic value < the dividend, and report the count. Do not claim the backtest prices this risk.
22. PASSIVE EXECUTION IS OUT OF SCOPE: the simulator may only model marketable/liquidity-taking orders. Any strategy config specifying resting limit orders, mid-price fills, or price improvement is rejected at validation time with the message that fill probability cannot be estimated from trade prints.
23. SIZE CAP: since historical depth is unknowable, cap simulated quantity at a hard configured constant per contract class (record it as an assumption in the report) and never let modelled size scale with historical volume.
24. BUILD THE CALIBRATION SET FORWARD, STARTING NOW: the live/paper bot must persist an NBBO snapshot (bid, ask, bid_size, ask_size, timestamp) at every order submission and at every fill, plus 1-minute NBBO for all traded contracts. Backtests covering dates before recording began are labelled 'uncalibrated'. Only recorded NBBO or real live fills may be used to fit the spread model -- paper fills may not, because Alpaca's simulator fills against NBBO with no size check and random partials.
25. CLAIMS BLOCK: every report must emit the explicit entitled/not-entitled claims block. A report writer must refuse to emit the strings 'expected return', 'Sharpe', 'profit factor', 'win rate' or 'drawdown' as unqualified estimates while assumed_cost=true; those metrics may only appear with the 1x/2x/4x sensitivity table adjacent and the word UPPER-BOUND on the headline number.
26. COMPARISONS NEED A MARGIN: two strategy variants may not be ranked unless their net-P&L difference exceeds the spread-model uncertainty band (the 1x-to-2x spread range). Otherwise the tuner must report 'indistinguishable' rather than picking a winner.

## 0DTE options strategy performance on SPX/SPY/QQQ -- what the published evidence actually supports, and the hard constraints it implies for an automated Alpaca options-L3 system

1. INSTRUMENT WHITELIST: 0DTE short-premium entries are permitted only on SPY, QQQ, IWM. Hard-reject any 0DTE multi-leg short-premium order on a single-name underlying (including all MAG7), on grounds of negative VRP skew (OptionMetrics: single-name VRP skew -0.45 to -4.14 vs SPX +1.33). Implement as an allowlist, not a scoring penalty.
2. NO EXPIRATION CARRY, EVER: every 0DTE position must be fully flat by 15:00 ET. Emit a WARN at 14:45 ET if any 0DTE position is open, force an unconditional market-crossing close attempt at 15:00 ET, and treat failure to be flat by 15:20 ET as a P1 alert plus a trading halt for the rest of the day. Never allow the strategy to rely on Alpaca's 15:45 ET auto-liquidation or on expiry settlement.
3. RESPECT ALPACA CUTOFFS AS HARD CONSTANTS: no order submission after 15:30 ET for SPY/QQQ/IWM (15:15 ET for single names) -- orders are rejected by the broker. Store these as named constants with the source URL in a comment, and assert at startup that the configured flatten time is at least 30 minutes before them.
4. PIN-RISK GUARD: from 14:00 ET onward, on every cycle, if spot is within max(1.0 x current ATR(5min), 0.5 x wing width) of ANY short strike in an open position, close that position immediately at market-crossing limits regardless of P&L. Log the event. Rationale: partial-ITM assignment converts a defined-risk spread into a naked overnight 100-share position whose loss is unbounded (documented example: -$800 on a $4-wide spread).
5. NEVER CREATE AN UNCOVERED SHORT: all opens and closes must be submitted as a single MLeg order containing every leg. If a partial fill or a broker rejection ever leaves a short leg without its cover, immediately close the short leg first (never the long leg first) and raise a P1 alert. Assert before every order that no permutation of partial execution leaves a naked short. Alpaca rejects uncovered short strategies outright, so a naked leg means the position cannot be managed normally.
6. PRE-TRADE COST GATE (highest-priority check): compute modeled round-trip cost = sum over legs of (ask-bid)/2, times 2 for entry+exit, plus per-contract fees (ORF $0.015 both sides, OCC $0.025 both sides, TAF $0.00329 sells, CAT negligible). Reject the entry if modeled_round_trip_cost / net_credit > 0.15. Log the computed ratio on every entry, accepted or rejected. Quote must be a live NBBO snapshot less than 2 seconds old.
7. NO MARKET ORDERS ON MULTI-LEG 0DTE, EVER, INCLUDING EXITS. Use limit MLeg orders. Entry: start at mid, reprice toward the natural at most 3 times at 5-second intervals, then cancel. Exit: start at mid, step toward the natural every 3 seconds, but cross the full spread if not filled within 30 seconds -- an unfilled 0DTE exit is a larger risk than the spread cost.
8. STOP-LIMIT, NOT STOP-LOSS: every protective exit must be a stop-limit with the limit placed a defined buffer (minimum 3 ticks) beyond the stop, never a bare stop. Documented practitioner failure mode: in a liquidity vacuum a market stop fills at outrageous prices. Additionally, log every stop trigger and compute a rolling false-stop rate (stops that would have recovered); if false-stop rate exceeds 50% over 40 trades, raise the stop distance or halt the strategy for review.
9. SIZE ON TAIL LOSS, NOT ON SPREAD MAX LOSS: per-position underlying notional must satisfy 0.0158 x notional <= 0.5% of account equity, using the published 1% expected-shortfall upper bound of 1.58% of underlying. On a $50k account this caps notional at roughly $15,800 per position (about 25 SPY shares-equivalent, i.e. well under one 1-lot at SPY ~$600 -- meaning the honest output of this rule is that $50k supports at most one small defined-risk spread at a time). Do not override this with the nominal max loss of the spread.
10. PORTFOLIO CAPS: total simultaneous defined max loss across all open 0DTE positions <= 2% of account equity; single-position defined max loss <= 0.5% of equity. Additionally cap the number of concurrent 0DTE positions at 2, and forbid two positions with overlapping short strikes on the same underlying (the Option Alpha dataset explicitly warns its results ignored overlapping strikes and portfolio risk).
11. BREAK-EVEN WIN-RATE MONITOR: at entry compute required_win_rate = max_loss / (max_loss + net_credit). Persist it. Maintain a rolling realized win rate over the trailing 60 closed trades. If realized_win_rate < required_win_rate (averaged over those trades) for 10 consecutive trading days, halt the strategy and require human re-approval. This is the check that would have caught the published iron-fly case (78% realized vs 88% required in 2025).
12. VARIANCE-EDGE PRECONDITION: do not open a short-premium 0DTE position unless entry ATM implied vol exceeds a forecast of today's intraday realized vol by a configured minimum margin (start at 1.0 vol point). Use a documented forecast (e.g. prior-day intraday squared-log-return realized vol, the OptionMetrics methodology) and log implied, forecast, and the realized outcome for every trade so the premise can be tested. If the margin cannot be computed, do not trade. Rationale: delta-hedged short-option P&L = 0.5 x Gamma x S^2 x (realized_var - implied_var) x dt; there is no other source of edge.
13. INTRADAY LOSS CIRCUIT BREAKERS: halt all new entries and close all open 0DTE positions if any of the following fire -- (a) cumulative day P&L <= -1.5% of account equity; (b) two consecutive trades hit full stop; (c) SPY moves more than 1.5% from the prior close intraday; (d) SPY 5-minute return exceeds 4 standard deviations of its trailing 20-day 5-minute distribution. Weekly halt at -4% of equity requiring human re-approval to resume.
14. EVENT BLACKOUT: no 0DTE entries on FOMC decision days before 14:30 ET, and none within 30 minutes either side of scheduled CPI, PPI, NFP, or PCE releases. Maintain the calendar as data, fail closed (no trade) if the calendar is stale by more than 24 hours.
15. ENTRY WINDOW IS A RISK CONTROL, NOT ALPHA: permit entries only between 10:00 and 13:30 ET. Do not let any optimizer tune the entry clock time, day-of-week filter, or expiry weekday -- the published evidence that these carry expectancy is in-sample and uncorrected for multiple testing. Hard-code the window and record it as a risk decision.
16. BAN DEALER-GAMMA / GEX SIGNALS from entry logic. Cboe's own data shows median dealer net 0DTE gamma of +$173mm at 15:30 (0.04-0.17% of S&P futures liquidity) and market makers left holding 3% of gross volume; Dim/Eraker/Vilkov find 0DTE gamma does not propagate volatility. Any commercial gamma-flow feed may be logged for research but must not gate an order.
17. BACKTESTS MUST BE MARKED TO EXECUTABLE PRICES: any backtest used to justify a parameter change must fill sells at the bid and buys at the ask (not mid), include the full fee schedule, and simulate the 15:00 ET forced flatten. A mid-marked backtest must be rejected by the research pipeline with an explicit error. The published evidence is that iron butterflies flip from gross Sharpe 0.77 to negative purely on cost treatment.
18. ASSIGNMENT DETECTION AND RECOVERY: after every fill and at 16:15 ET and 09:25 ET daily, reconcile Alpaca positions against the internal ledger. If an unexpected equity position appears (assignment), raise a P1 alert immediately, block all new option entries, and flatten the shares at the next regular-session open with a limit order. Never let the bot silently carry assigned shares.
19. DO NOT EXTRAPOLATE SPX RESEARCH TO SPY/QQQ WITHOUT A HAIRCUT: every parameter imported from a published SPX study must be tagged in config with its source and a flag indicating it is unvalidated on physically-settled ETFs. Require a minimum 100-trade paper track record on the actual SPY/QQQ implementation, with realized win rate, realized payoff ratio, realized cost-to-credit ratio, and realized IV-minus-RV all logged, before any parameter is promoted to a live-capital configuration. There is no published study of 0DTE short premium on SPY/QQQ net of assignment.
20. NO SIZE ESCALATION AFTER LOSSES: position size must be a pure function of account equity and the tail-loss rule above. Explicitly forbid martingale, averaging down into a losing 0DTE structure, rolling out a tested side, or adding a second position to 'repair' the first. Note also that Alpaca's MLeg coverage requirement makes rolling a short contract impossible in a single order, so any repair logic would transiently create a naked short.
21. VERIFY, DO NOT ASSUME, REGULATORY STATE AT STARTUP: query the account's actual day-trade / PDT status, options level, and buying power from the Alpaca API on every start rather than assuming the PDT repeal or level 3 approval applies. Fail closed and refuse to trade if options_approved_level < 3, if the account is flagged in any restricted state, or if the API call fails.
22. HUMAN GATE ON REAL MONEY: this system stays on the paper account. Any transition to live capital requires an explicit, separate human action outside the codebase -- no configuration flag, environment variable, or agent decision may switch the account endpoint. Assert at startup that the base URL is the paper endpoint and abort otherwise.

## Computing option greeks and implied volatility locally when the broker supplies none, with correct handling of 0DTE

1. Never require broker-supplied greeks. Alpaca returns none when expiration is today, when either side of the contract quote is zero, when there is no latest SIP underlying trade, or when its 100-iteration IV solve fails. The local engine is the source of truth; broker greeks may only be logged as a cross-check and must never be substituted in when the local value is missing.
2. Define exactly two clock constants and use one globally per computation path: MINUTES_PER_CALENDAR_YEAR = 525600 and MINUTES_PER_BUSINESS_YEAR = 102060 (252 * 6.75 * 60). Use the business clock for all 0DTE and short-dated work. Every stored or transmitted IV must carry the clock tag; any comparison, ratio, or term-structure calculation between two IVs with different clock tags must raise, not coerce.
3. Compute T from a per-symbol expiry instant read from configuration, defaulting to 16:00:00 America/New_York, not 16:15. Clamp T to zero at or past that instant. Assert T > 0 before any log or division; if T <= 0 return terminal-payoff values (delta = +/-1 or 0 by intrinsic, gamma = vega = theta = 0) tagged 'expired', and never feed them to a solver.
4. Solve for total variance w = sigma*sqrt(T), never for sigma directly. Use the derivative dPrice/dw = S*exp(-q*T)*phi(d1) (which stays bounded as T -> 0) and recover sigma = w/sqrt(T) only at the end. A solver whose state variable is sigma and whose derivative is vega = S*exp(-q*T)*phi(d1)*sqrt(T) must be rejected in code review.
5. Before solving, validate the target mid against European no-arbitrage bounds and refuse rather than solve if violated: calls must satisfy max(0, S*exp(-q*T) - K*exp(-r*T)) < mid < S*exp(-q*T); puts must satisfy max(0, K*exp(-r*T) - S*exp(-q*T)) < mid < K*exp(-r*T). A violation returns reason code PRICE_OUTSIDE_BOUNDS.
6. Route every IV solve to the out-of-the-money contract: solve the put when K < F, the call when K >= F, and derive the in-the-money twin via put-call parity C - P = exp(-r*T)*(F - K). Never solve IV directly from an in-the-money American quote.
7. Obtain the forward F from put-call parity at the strike where |call_mid - put_mid| is smallest, using F = K + exp(r*T)*(call_mid - put_mid), and price with Black-76. Do not model a dividend yield q for SPY/QQQ. If both ATM legs do not have two-sided, uncrossed, non-stale quotes, fall back to F = S*exp(r*T) and set a degraded-forward flag on every downstream greek.
8. Use a bracketed, safeguarded solver, never bare Newton. Bracket w in [1e-8, w_hi] where w_hi is doubled from an initial guess until the model price exceeds the target or the implied sigma exceeds 5.0 (500%). Accept a Newton step only if it lands strictly inside the current bracket, otherwise bisect. Terminate on |model_price - target| < 0.25 * tick or bracket width < 1e-10, with a hard cap of 50 iterations. Iteration-cap exhaustion returns reason code NO_CONVERGENCE, not the last iterate.
9. Compute N(x) = 0.5*erfc(-x/sqrt(2)). Polynomial CDF approximations are prohibited. Guard phi(d1) underflow: when phi(d1) == 0.0, set gamma = 0 and vega = 0 explicitly rather than evaluating the ratio, and assert no NaN or Inf leaves the greek function.
10. Compute and store an IV uncertainty band for every solved IV: iv_band = (ask_mid_half_spread) / vega_per_unit_vol, where vega_per_unit_vol = S*exp(-q*T)*phi(d1)*sqrt(T). Any strategy decision that depends on the IV level (richness, edge, vol-based sizing) must be blocked when iv_band exceeds a configured threshold; a sane starting threshold is 0.02 (2 vol points). Delta/gamma-based decisions are exempt because they are clock- and IV-band-insensitive when IV was solved from the same quote.
11. Reject the quote before solving if any of: bid <= 0, ask <= 0, bid > ask, quote age > a configured staleness limit (start at 2 seconds intraday), or the option quote timestamp and the underlying quote timestamp differ by more than that limit. Each rejection returns its own reason code.
12. Use the underlying's NBBO mid, not the last trade, as S, so that S and the option mid are measured consistently. Record both timestamps with every greek row.
13. Enforce a last-hour regime following Cboe's own VIX1D rule: once business minutes remaining < 60, stop accepting newly solved IV as an input to sizing and instead freeze the last IV solved while minutes >= 60. Position risk in this window must be evaluated from terminal payoff and distance-to-strike, not from theta or vega.
14. Implement a model-free fallback for probability-of-ITM when the IV solve fails but adjacent same-expiry strikes have two-sided quotes: N(d2) ~ exp(r*T) * (C(K - dK) - C(K + dK)) / (2*dK). Tag the result as MODEL_FREE_PROXY and mark vega, theta and gamma as unavailable (None, never 0) for that contract.
15. Never fabricate a greek. When IV cannot be solved and the model-free proxy is also unavailable, return None for every greek, block all new entries on that contract, and mark existing positions with worst-case risk assumptions (delta = +/-1.0 if intrinsic > 0, 0.0 otherwise) for margin and sizing purposes. A zero must never stand in for a missing greek.
16. Never interpolate IV across expirations. When filling a missing IV, interpolate only along the same expiry, in log-moneyness ln(K/F) versus total variance w, and only between strikes whose own solves succeeded and whose iv_band passed the threshold. Refuse to extrapolate beyond the outermost successful strike.
17. Source the risk-free rate from the U.S. Treasury daily par yield curve (CMT), interpolate with a natural cubic spline on days-to-maturity, and convert BEY -> APY -> continuous as APY = (1 + BEY/2)^2 - 1 then r = ln(1 + APY). Cache per business day. If the rate is older than one business day, allow the 0DTE path to continue (rho * 100bp is about 1.2 cents/share at T = 1 day) but block any 30-45 DTE pricing decision (the same error is about 37 cents/share at 45 DTE) or force it onto the parity-implied forward instead.
18. Do not implement a binomial or American solver. Instead compute and log the two early-exercise error bounds on every valuation: american_call_premium <= PV(dividends with ex-date strictly before expiry), and american_put_premium <= K*(1 - exp(-r*T)). If either bound exceeds a configured fraction of the option's mid (start at 2%), flag the valuation as EUROPEAN_APPROX_SUSPECT rather than silently using it.
19. Maintain a hard-coded, validated ex-dividend calendar for every ETF traded. SPY 2026 ex-dates are 2026-03-20, 2026-06-18, 2026-09-18, 2026-12-18 (plus a potential 2026-12-31 excise distribution). The calendar must be re-fetched from the issuer at least quarterly and the fetch failing must raise, not default to 'no dividends'. QQQ's schedule is NOT verified here and must be fetched from Invesco before any QQQ short-call position is opened.
20. On the last trading session before any ex-dividend date, screen every short call leg: compute remaining_time_value = call_mid - max(0, S - K); if dividend_per_share > remaining_time_value, close the leg or explicitly provision for assignment before the close. This check is independent of the greek engine and must run even when IV solves fail.
21. Stop opening or extending 0DTE positions no later than 15:25 ET, ahead of Alpaca's documented 15:30 ET cutoff on expiration day. The gate must be wall-clock based and must not depend on any model output.
22. Run a pre-close expiration sweep that evaluates every expiring position against intrinsic value with a $0.01 threshold (the OCC exercise-by-exception level Alpaca applies), verifies that buying power for call exercise or shares for put exercise are actually present, and closes or submits DNE otherwise. Do not rely on delta to predict whether a contract finishes ITM; use the $0.01 intrinsic test directly.
23. Every greek row written to storage must carry: the clock tag, T in years, the forward source (parity or spot-compounded), the rate and its as-of date, the IV solve reason code, the iv_band, the underlying and option quote timestamps, and a degraded/fallback flag. A row missing any of these fields must not be consumed by the strategy layer.
24. Add regression tests that assert the clock invariance identity: for a fixed market mid, solving IV under the calendar clock and under the business clock must produce spot deltas and gammas agreeing to within 1e-6, and IVs whose ratio equals sqrt(525600/102060) to within 1e-6. A failure of this test means the solver and the greek functions are not using the same T.

## Alpaca options trading API -- precise mechanics for an automated level-3 paper system (US equity/ETF options)

1. SYMBOLS -- Never trade a symbol the bot composed. The only tradable symbol string is one returned by GET /v2/options/contracts (or /v2/options/contracts/{symbol_or_id}). Composition is permitted only to build a filter.
2. SYMBOLS -- Parse OCC symbols from the RIGHT: strike = int(sym[-8:]) / 1000; right = 'C' or 'P' at sym[-9]; expiry = datetime.strptime(sym[-15:-9], '%y%m%d'); root = sym[:-15]. Assert len(sym) >= 16 and sym[-9] in 'CP' and sym[-8:].isdigit(). Never pad the root to 6 chars; never index from the left.
3. CONTRACTS -- Every contracts query must explicitly set expiration_date_gte AND expiration_date_lte (the server default silently clips to 'next weekend') and limit (default 100, max 10000), and must loop on pagination reading `next_page_token or page_token`, stopping on null/empty, with a hard cap on iterations.
4. CONTRACTS -- Reject any contract where tradable is not true or status != 'active'. Reject any contract where style != 'american' unless the strategy explicitly handles cash settlement.
5. CONTRACTS -- Reject any contract where multiplier != '100' or size != multiplier, unless an explicit 'allow_adjusted_contracts' flag is set. Use contract.multiplier (never size, never a hardcoded 100) in every premium, cost-basis and max-loss calculation.
6. ORDER BUILDER -- Whitelist per asset class before transmit. For options: reject notional, reject fractional qty (assert qty == int(qty) and qty >= 1), reject extended_hours=True, reject time_in_force not in {'day','gtc'}, reject order type not in {'market','limit','stop','stop_limit'}.
7. ORDER BUILDER -- For order_class='mleg': reject type not in {'market','limit'}; reject stop_price/trail_price/trail_percent; reject take_profit/stop_loss blocks. Bracket/OCO/OTO are equity-only and must be rejected for any option order.
8. MLEG -- Assert 2 <= len(legs) <= 4, all leg symbols distinct, all legs share the same underlying_symbol, and gcd(all ratio_qty) == 1 (normalize by dividing ratios by the GCD and multiplying parent qty by it).
9. MLEG -- Omit top-level `symbol` and `side` entirely; set top-level `qty` (required, integer >= 1, = number of strategy units). Executed leg contracts = qty * ratio_qty; assert that product against sizing limits per leg, not just per order.
10. MLEG -- Set `position_intent` explicitly on EVERY leg (buy_to_open | buy_to_close | sell_to_open | sell_to_close). Derive it from the bot's own position ledger, then assert it agrees with the live /v2/positions state before transmit, to avoid 'position intent mismatch'.
11. MLEG PRICING -- Compute net price as sum over legs of (sign * ratio_qty * leg_price), sign = +1 for buy, -1 for sell. Submit limit_price with that sign convention: POSITIVE = debit, NEGATIVE = credit. Assert sign(limit_price) == expected sign for the strategy (credit spreads MUST send a negative limit_price) and refuse to transmit on mismatch.
12. MLEG PRICING -- Bound the limit price: refuse to submit if abs(limit_price) exceeds the strategy's theoretical max (e.g. a vertical's net debit must be < strike_width) or if the quoted net bid/ask spread exceeds a configured max (reject illiquid structures rather than crossing a wide market).
13. LEVEL 3 GATE -- Read account.options_trading_level (the EFFECTIVE min of approved and max_options_trading_level) at startup and at least once per session. Halt all option activity if it is < the level the running strategy requires. Never key off options_approved_level.
14. LEVEL 3 GATE -- Hard-reject, in code, before order construction: any naked/uncovered short option; any short straddle or strangle; any single-leg sell_to_open that is not (a) a covered call with >= 100*qty shares in qty_available of the underlying, or (b) a cash-secured put with strike*multiplier*qty of options_buying_power reserved.
15. LEVEL 3 GATE -- For every mleg order, verify coverage INSIDE that order: each short leg must be matched by a long leg of the same underlying, the same expiration date, and a protective strike (long call strike <= short call strike offset for debit structures; long put strike <= short put strike; condors: both wings present). Reject calendars, diagonals, and 'roll a naked short' as mleg -- Alpaca rejects them and a partial workaround by legging must be an explicit, separately-flagged code path.
16. LEVEL 3 GATE -- Do not put an equity leg in an mleg order. The docs contradict themselves on whether it is allowed; treat it as forbidden. Buy-writes must be two separate orders with explicit leg-risk handling.
17. PERMISSIONS DISCOVERY -- Maintain a persisted per-strategy-family whitelist populated by one-contract probe orders in paper, keyed on (status, code) of the response. Treat 403/40310000 with an eligibility message as a permanent disable for that family until a human re-enables it; never retry it in a loop.
18. CAPITAL -- Size from account.options_buying_power, not buying_power. Reserve, per order, at least the universal-spread-rule maintenance margin plus the net debit (credits reduce it), and re-read options_buying_power after every fill rather than decrementing a local estimate.
19. CAPITAL -- Compute pre-trade risk as the worst-case piecewise net payoff of (existing positions + proposed position) evaluated at every strike present, per expiration date, taking the max requirement across expirations -- not as the standalone max loss of the new spread. Block the order if that portfolio worst case breaches the configured account-level loss cap.
20. ERRORS -- Classify on (HTTP status, numeric code) only: 403/40310000 = permission or buying power, 422/42210000 = validation, 40410000 = not found. Never regex the message. Extract structured extras (options_buying_power, cost_basis, required_qty, available_qty, required_options_buying_power) from the JSON body for logging and sizing.
21. ERRORS -- Treat `submit mleg order is disabled`, `cancel mleg order is disabled`, `replace mleg order is disabled` (403) as kill-switch conditions: stop the strategy, alert, do not retry.
22. CLOCK -- Gate every option order on the market clock/calendar. Market orders: refuse outside 09:30-16:00 ET (Alpaca rejects them). Limit/day orders: refuse after the close unless the strategy explicitly intends a next-session order, because a day order submitted after close is queued to the following trading day.
23. CLOCK -- Hard stop: do not submit any opening or position-extending order on a contract expiring today after 15:30 ET. Alpaca stops accepting them and the bot's own state will diverge.
24. EXITS -- Never assume a broker-side stop exists on a multi-leg position. Every spread must be paired with a bot-side monitor that submits a closing mleg order (or per-leg orders) when its exit condition triggers, with a heartbeat that alerts if the monitor stops running while the position is open.
25. EXITS -- Prefer cancel-then-resubmit over PATCH replace for re-pricing option orders; treat replace as best-effort and handle 'order parameters are not changed', 'order chain not fully replaced', 'qty must be > filled_qty' as non-fatal no-ops.
26. POSITIONS -- Identify option positions by asset_class == 'us_option'. qty is CONTRACTS. market_value already includes the multiplier; current_price does not. Never multiply market_value by 100. Expiry/strike/type are absent from the position object -- get them from the contract record.
27. POSITIONS -- Always check qty_available (not qty) before submitting a closing/covering order; shares and contracts locked against a covered call or an open order are excluded from qty_available.
28. RECONCILIATION -- Detect exercise, assignment and expiry by diffing /v2/positions and /v2/account against the bot's expected state on a schedule, NOT by waiting for activities or websocket events: assignments are never pushed over websocket, and on paper all option NTAs appear only the next day.
29. RECONCILIATION -- After 15:30 ET on any expiration date the bot holds, poll positions at least every few minutes until flat: Alpaca may auto-exercise, auto-assign, or force-liquidate positions with no order from the bot, including positions 'slightly OTM'.
30. EXERCISE -- Do not call POST /v2/positions/{symbol_or_contract_id}/exercise as a routine exit; close by trading the contract. If exercising at all: require an explicit config flag, verify the position is long, American-style, ITM, inside market hours, and that cash/regt buying power covers strike*multiplier*full position qty -- the call has no qty parameter and exercises the ENTIRE position irreversibly.
31. 0DTE -- Handle 'exceeded account limit for contracts expiring today' (403/40310000) as a hard per-account cap whose value Alpaca does not publish: parse the returned limit, persist it, and cap same-day-expiry contract counts below it thereafter. Do not retry blindly.
32. PAPER -- Log requested vs filled net price and fill latency on every option order, and treat paper fill quality as an upper bound, not evidence. Alpaca's paper engine does not check size against NBBO depth, partial-fills randomly ~10% of the time, and has no documented multi-leg fill model. Do not size live capital from paper option P&L.
33. IDEMPOTENCY -- Set a deterministic client_order_id (<= 128 chars) on every option order, derived from strategy + underlying + expiry + strikes + intent + timestamp bucket, so a retry after a timeout cannot double-open a spread.
